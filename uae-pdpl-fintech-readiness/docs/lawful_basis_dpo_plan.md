# Lawful Basis and DPO Appointment Plan

## Lawful Basis Register

| Data Element | Lawful Basis | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| Emirates ID number | Legal obligation | Required for identity verification, KYC, AML, and other applicable regulatory compliance obligations. |
| Full name | Contract necessity | Required to establish and administer the customer's financial-services relationship. |
| Phone number | Contract necessity | Required for account communications, authentication, transaction alerts, and service delivery. |
| Transaction history | Legal obligation | Maintained to support financial recordkeeping, AML monitoring, fraud investigation, and regulatory obligations. |
| IP address | Legitimate interest | Used to detect suspicious access patterns, investigate abuse, and protect platform security. |
| KYC documents | Legal obligation | Required to verify customer identity and satisfy applicable KYC and AML compliance requirements. |
| Bank account number | Contract necessity | Required to execute settlements, deposits, withdrawals, and other customer-requested financial transactions. |
| Device fingerprint | Legitimate interest | Used for fraud prevention, account protection, anomaly detection, and security risk assessment. |

## Lawful Basis Governance Notes

- Each processing activity must have a documented purpose before personal data is collected or used.
- Personal data must not be reused for an incompatible purpose without evaluating whether another lawful basis is required.
- Consent should be used only where processing genuinely depends on a freely given and revocable choice.
- Legal and compliance teams should periodically reassess whether the selected lawful basis remains valid as products and processing activities change.

## DPO Appointment Plan

### Trigger Criteria Met?

**Yes — based on the assumed operating model of this fintech platform.**

The platform processes identity-verification information, Emirates ID data, financial information, KYC documents, transaction records, device information, and fraud-risk indicators. At production scale, these activities may involve large volumes of sensitive or high-impact personal data and systematic monitoring or profiling of customers for fraud and risk purposes.

Accordingly, the organization should treat the Article 10 DPO appointment requirement as applicable for this compliance-readiness scenario.

### Proposed DPO Reporting Line

The Data Protection Officer should:

- Report directly to executive management or another sufficiently senior governance function.
- Maintain independence from teams that determine the purposes and means of personal-data processing.
- Have direct escalation access to senior management and relevant risk or audit committees.
- Coordinate closely with Legal, Compliance, Information Security, Engineering, Risk, and Internal Audit.

### Key DPO Responsibilities

1. Monitor compliance with UAE personal-data protection requirements and internal privacy policies.
2. Advise business and technical teams on privacy risks, controls, impact assessments, and high-risk processing.
3. Oversee data-subject rights requests, consent governance, retention practices, and breach-response coordination.
4. Act as a central privacy contact point for the UAE Data Office, data subjects, management, and relevant internal stakeholders.

### Anticipated Data Office Registration Steps

1. Confirm whether controller, processor, DPO, or other registration requirements apply under current UAE regulatory procedures.
2. Compile the organization's legal identity, processing activities, contact details, and DPO information.
3. Submit required information through the mechanism designated by the competent UAE authority when applicable.
4. Maintain evidence of registration, submissions, correspondence, and any regulatory reference numbers.
5. Update submitted information when material organizational, processing, or DPO details change.

## Governance Review

This register should be reviewed:

- When a new fintech product or processing purpose is introduced.
- When new sensitive or high-risk personal-data categories are collected.
- When fraud-monitoring, profiling, AI, or automated decision-making capabilities materially change.
- When UAE privacy regulations, executive regulations, or regulator guidance are updated.
