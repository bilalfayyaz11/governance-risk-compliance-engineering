# Breach Notification Procedure

## 1. Detection & Escalation

- Any employee, contractor, monitoring system, or service provider that detects a suspected personal-data breach must immediately report it to Information Security and the Data Protection Officer.
- Security Operations must begin initial triage as soon as the incident is identified, preserving logs, evidence, affected-system details, and the estimated time of compromise.
- The DPO, Legal, Compliance, Security, and relevant system owner must be notified internally within 4 hours of confirming that personal data may be involved.
- The incident must be assigned a severity level, incident owner, investigation lead, and documented timeline in the breach register.

## 2. Risk Assessment Criteria

The response team must assess whether the breach is likely to create significant or high risk to affected individuals.

- Determine the sensitivity of the affected data, including Emirates ID information, KYC documents, bank-account details, credentials, transaction data, or other financial information.
- Assess the number of affected individuals, volume of records, duration of exposure, and whether the information was encrypted, tokenized, or otherwise protected.
- Evaluate likely consequences such as identity theft, financial fraud, account takeover, profiling, reputational harm, discrimination, or unauthorized disclosure.
- Consider whether the attacker or unauthorized recipient accessed, copied, altered, disclosed, or exfiltrated the information.

## 3. Regulator Notification (UAE Data Office)

- The DPO and Legal team must determine whether notification to the competent UAE data-protection authority is required based on applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
- For this readiness workflow, the organization adopts an internal target of completing regulator assessment and, where required, preparing notification within 72 hours of confirmed breach discovery.
- The notification package should include the nature of the breach, affected data categories, estimated number of individuals affected, likely consequences, containment measures, remediation actions, and DPO contact information.
- If complete information is not yet available, the organization should document known facts, outstanding investigation items, and provide supplementary information as it becomes available where permitted.

## 4. Data Subject Notification

Affected individuals should be notified when the incident assessment determines that the breach creates a significant risk to their rights, privacy, identity, finances, or account security.

Notification should:

- Use clear, non-technical language and identify the types of personal data affected.
- Explain the likely consequences of the breach and the actions already taken by the organization.
- Give practical protective steps such as password changes, account monitoring, fraud alerts, identity verification precautions, or contacting financial institutions.
- Provide DPO or privacy-contact information for questions and further assistance.

### Template Message

**Subject: Important Notice Regarding Your Personal Data**

We identified a security incident involving personal information associated with your account.

The affected information may include: **[DATA CATEGORIES]**.

We identified the incident on **[DATE]** and immediately began containment and investigation activities. We have taken steps including **[CONTAINMENT ACTIONS]** to reduce further risk.

Potential risks may include **[LIKELY CONSEQUENCES]**.

We recommend that you:

1. Monitor your account and financial activity for unusual transactions.
2. Change affected passwords or credentials where applicable.
3. Contact us immediately if you observe suspicious activity.
4. Follow any additional protective instructions included in this notice.

For privacy-related questions, contact:

**Data Protection Officer**  
Email: **[DPO CONTACT]**

## 5. Post-Incident Review

- Conduct a formal root-cause analysis covering the technical failure, process failure, control weakness, or third-party issue that enabled the breach.
- Record lessons learned, corrective actions, responsible owners, deadlines, and evidence of remediation in the breach register.
- Review access controls, logging, retention, encryption, monitoring, vendor controls, and incident-response procedures affected by the event.
- Present significant incidents and remediation status to appropriate executive, risk, audit, or governance stakeholders.

## Breach Response Timeline

| Time From Detection | Required Action |
|---|---|
| 0-1 hour | Detect, contain, preserve evidence, alert Security Operations |
| Within 4 hours | Escalate to DPO, Legal, Compliance, and system owner |
| Within 24 hours | Complete initial impact and personal-data risk assessment |
| Within 48 hours | Determine notification requirements and prepare evidence |
| Internal target: within 72 hours | Submit regulator notification if legally required and sufficiently established |
| As soon as required | Notify affected individuals where risk threshold is met |
| After containment | Perform root-cause review and corrective-action tracking |

## Required Breach Record

Every personal-data breach record should document:

- Incident identifier
- Discovery date and time
- Systems affected
- Data categories affected
- Number of potentially affected individuals
- Geographic scope
- Root cause
- Threat actor or unauthorized recipient, if known
- Containment actions
- Risk assessment
- Regulator notification decision
- Data subject notification decision
- Corrective actions
- Closure date
