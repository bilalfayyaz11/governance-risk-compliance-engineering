SYSTEM: Patient Appointment Scheduling System

Information Type 1:
NIST Reference: D.14.1 - Access to Care
Description: Appointment dates, appointment times, provider assignments,
scheduling status, and information used to coordinate patient access to care.

Final Impact:
Confidentiality: Low
Integrity: Moderate
Availability: Moderate

Adjustment:
Availability raised from Low to Moderate because prolonged disruption of
scheduling across the clinic network could seriously affect timely access
to care and continuity of patient services.


Information Type 2:
NIST Reference: C.2.8.9 - Personal Identity and Authentication
Description: Patient names, phone numbers, email addresses, and identifying
information used to associate appointments with the correct individuals.

Final Impact:
Confidentiality: Moderate
Integrity: Moderate
Availability: Moderate

Adjustment:
Moderate confidentiality is retained because unauthorized disclosure of
patient identity and contact information creates privacy impact. Integrity
and availability remain Moderate based on the provisional information-type
categorization and the operational importance of accurate identity data.


SOURCE:
NIST SP 800-60 Volume 2 Revision 1
FIPS Publication 199
