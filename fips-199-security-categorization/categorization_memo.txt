PURPOSE

This memo documents the security categorization of the Patient Appointment
Scheduling System using FIPS 199 and NIST SP 800-60 guidance.

INFORMATION TYPES IDENTIFIED

1. D.14.1 - Access to Care
   Confidentiality: Low
   Integrity: Moderate
   Availability: Moderate

2. C.2.8.9 - Personal Identity and Authentication
   Confidentiality: Moderate
   Integrity: Moderate
   Availability: Moderate

OVERALL SYSTEM CATEGORIZATION
(FIPS 199 High-Water Mark)

Confidentiality: Moderate
Integrity: Moderate
Availability: Moderate

Security Categorization:
SC = {(confidentiality, MODERATE),
      (integrity, MODERATE),
      (availability, MODERATE)}

JUSTIFICATION SUMMARY

The system processes appointment scheduling information together with patient
names and basic contact information.

Confidentiality is categorized as Moderate because unauthorized disclosure of
patient identity and contact information could create a serious privacy impact.

Integrity is categorized as Moderate because inaccurate or unauthorized
modification of identity or scheduling information could cause incorrect
appointments, provider assignments, or disruption to patient access to care.

Availability is categorized as Moderate because prolonged loss of the
scheduling capability across the clinic network could seriously interfere with
timely access to care and continuity of patient services.

Using the FIPS 199 high-water-mark method, the highest impact value for each
security objective is Moderate.

RECOMMENDATION

Categorize the Patient Appointment Scheduling System as a MODERATE-impact
information system and use this categorization as an input to subsequent
security control selection and risk management activities.
